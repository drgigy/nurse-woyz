Dictation Principle

The nurse should speak naturally. The system should not force a rigid form during dictation. It should listen for clinically useful handover cues and then arrange them under standard headings.

No automatic fabrication Abnormal findings first Pending work visible Next nurse focused

Nursing Shift Handover - Sample Format

Identification

  • Patient name, room/bed, age, gender, UHID if available.
  • Primary reason for admission or current working problem if dictated.

Current Status

  • Present condition, sensorium, comfort, mobility, feeding, urine/stool, oxygen or device support.
  • Only mention normal findings if the nurse specifically states them.

Events / Changes This Shift

  • Fever, pain, breathlessness, deterioration, investigations sent, doctor informed, medication administered.
  • Patient response after nursing care or treatment if dictated.

WATCH

  • Risks or red flags the next nurse must monitor.
  • Escalation concerns such as altered sensorium, fall risk, respiratory distress, bleeding, urine output, or worsening pain.

NEXT SHIFT / PENDING

  • Pending investigations, reports, medications, procedures, review, transfer, or follow-up tasks.
  • What must be handed over clearly to avoid missed care.
