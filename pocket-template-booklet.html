How To Dictate
01

Speak naturally. Mention only what is present. The system must not add normal findings unless dictated.

  • Start with patient context: problem, current status, important changes.
  • Say the template area aloud if needed: "fall risk", "PASS ICU", "handover".
  • Use short findings: oxygen, lines, drains, mobility, feeding, urine, risk.
  • For unknown details say: "not checked" or "to verify".
  • Review the generated text before saving.

PASS ICU
02
  • 1:1 care indication or isolation requirement.

  • Dialysis, ICP monitoring, TPN, labs, dressings.
  • Urine output, diuresis, GRBS or insulin infusion.
